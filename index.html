Jiaxuan Li

李嘉烜

I am a Research Scientist at SB Intuitions Corp., where I work on large language models and vision-language models.

My research interests include multimodal reasoning, hallucination mitigation, knowledge grounding, and trustworthy AI.

I received my Ph.D. in Information Science and Technology from the University of Tokyo in 2025 under the supervision of Prof. Hideki Nakayama at the Nakayama Laboratory. Previously, I earned my M.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University under the supervision of Prof. Yuye Ling, and my B.Eng. from Shanghai University under the supervision of Prof. Zhi Liu.

I am open to research collaborations on multimodal and trustworthy AI. Please feel free to get in touch.
